# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep Qder and the restaurants and diners who use it safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **hello@qder.ai** with the subject "Security", or use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the
affected repository. Please include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect.

- We acknowledge reports within 3 business days and keep you updated until it is fixed.
- We ask for up to 90 days before public disclosure, and we credit reporters who want to be named.
- Please do not access data that is not yours, do not disrupt service, and do not test against restaurants or
  diners in production. Test accounts on request.

## Scope

The open-source repositories in this organization, and qder.ai, chat.qder.ai, dashboard.qder.ai and
status.qder.ai.
