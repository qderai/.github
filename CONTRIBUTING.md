# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome on our open-source repositories.

- **Bugs:** open an issue with steps to reproduce, what you expected and what happened.
- **Changes:** for anything beyond a small fix, open an issue first so we can agree on the approach.
- **Pull requests:** keep them focused, add or update tests, and make sure the repository's tests pass.
- **Security issues:** never in a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

By contributing you agree that your contribution is licensed under the repository's license.
